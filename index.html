About Me

Hi, I'm Matthew Johnson — a web developer based in Pensacola, FL. I take pride in building clean, professional websites that help businesses and individuals put their best foot forward online.

Before focusing on web development, I worked at Arby's where I was promoted to manager at age 18. That experience taught me accountability, leadership, and how to deliver results under pressure — qualities I bring to every project I take on.

I currently attend Pensacola Christian College, where I continue to sharpen my skills and grow both personally and professionally. My faith and strong work ethic are at the core of everything I do.

Whether you're a small business looking for your first website or an individual who needs a polished online presence, I'm here to help you get there.
